# Learning Plan Framework

Reference for building personalized bridge plans from existing knowledge to gap topics.

## Plan Structure

Every learning plan has three phases:

### Phase 1: Foundation
- **Goal**: Establish baseline understanding grounded in what you already know
- **Duration**: ~25% of total timeline
- **Activities**: Connect existing knowledge to new concepts, read foundational material, identify vocabulary and mental models
- **Checkpoint**: Exploratory-depth explore session

### Phase 2: Depth
- **Goal**: Build working understanding through practice and connection
- **Duration**: ~50% of total timeline
- **Activities**: Hands-on exercises, connect to actual projects, practice explaining concepts
- **Checkpoint**: 5-question quiz, target score ≥ 3.0/5

### Phase 3: Integration
- **Goal**: Achieve teaching-level understanding and practical application
- **Duration**: ~25% of total timeline
- **Activities**: Apply to novel scenarios, teach concepts, reflect on how this changes past decisions
- **Checkpoint**: Deep-depth explore session, target score ≥ 4.0/5

## Bridge Strategies

### From adjacent expertise
When the user has strong knowledge in a related area:
- Start with "what you already know about X also applies to Y because..."
- Identify shared principles and where they diverge
- Use familiar terminology as anchors for new concepts

### From practical experience
When the user has used the technology without understanding internals:
- Start with "you've done X — now let's understand why it works"
- Reverse-engineer from their experience to the underlying concepts
- Use their projects as case studies

### From no connection
When there's no clear bridge from existing knowledge:
- Start from fundamentals
- Use analogies to concepts they DO understand
- Build from first principles rather than from experience

## Timeline Templates

### 1-week sprint (~30 min/day)
- Days 1-2: Foundation
- Days 3-5: Depth
- Days 6-7: Integration

### 2-week steady (~20 min/day)
- Days 1-3: Foundation
- Days 4-10: Depth
- Days 11-14: Integration

### 1-month deep (~15 min/day)
- Week 1: Foundation
- Weeks 2-3: Depth
- Week 4: Integration

## Exercise Patterns

### Tied to real projects
- "How would [concept] change your approach to [past project]?"
- "Apply [technique] to [problem from your arsenal entry]"
- "Redesign [component from your project] using [new concept]"

### Teaching exercises
- "Write a 3-paragraph explanation of [concept] for a junior engineer"
- "Create a diagram showing how [concept A] relates to [concept B]"
- "Prepare a 5-minute walkthrough of [concept] using your experience as examples"

### Application exercises
- "Given [scenario], how would you apply [concept]?"
- "What questions would you ask before using [concept] in production?"
- "Compare [concept] to [alternative] — when would you choose each?"

## Success Criteria

A bridge plan is successful when the user can:
1. Explain the concept without notes or prompting
2. Identify when the concept applies and when it doesn't
3. Connect it to their existing experience meaningfully
4. Score ≥ 4/5 on a quiz for the topic
5. Teach the concept to someone else clearly
